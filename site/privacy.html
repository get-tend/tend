Privacy

The Tend app runs on your computer and sends nothing anywhere, unless you choose to add a purchase to the shared total. This page covers that and the website, gettend.eco.

What the site collects

Only what you type into the Startup waitlist or the letter to AI providers. Nothing else you do on the site is stored with your name.

Where it goes

Waitlist answers are stored in a private Google Sheet that only Tend can see, and used only to contact you about early access. Tend doesn't sell or share them.

The shared total

If you click "Add to the shared total" on a purchase in Tend, it sends that purchase's carbon and water amounts and its certificate link. Tend stores the amounts and a scrambled fingerprint of the link, so the same purchase can't be counted twice. It doesn't store the link itself, your name, your projects, or your usage.

The letter to AI providers

If you sign it, your name, email, and the optional role and company go to the same private Google Sheet. Your name and company are shown publicly only if you tick the box that says so.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no cookies. It counts page visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you.

Removing your details

Reply to any Tend email asking to be removed, or write to studio@gettend.eco and your details will be deleted.
