Ask AI providers to publish their footprint

Nobody outside AI companies knows the energy, carbon, or water of a single request. Tend estimates it with a range because that data isn't published. Add your name to ask for it.

To Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Meta, and every company serving AI models

We use your models every day, and we want to understand what that use costs the planet. Please publish, for each model:

  • Energy, carbon, and water per request, or per million tokens
  • Where requests are served, and the electricity mix there, hour by hour
  • Water used for cooling, and water used to generate that electricity
  • A regular schedule for these numbers, with methods others can check

With real numbers, people can choose models and providers with their footprint in mind, and projects like Tend can replace estimates with measurements.

Add your name

When the list is large enough, Tend will send it to each provider and publish their replies here.
