    How it works

    1. Track

      Reads token counts, models, and timestamps from Claude Code's local logs.

      Never your prompts, code, or files.
    2. Estimate

      Turns tokens into a low, middle, and high range for energy, carbon, and water.

      Updates after each session.
    3. Restore

      Shows what carbon removal and water restoration you could fund, and records what you do.

      You buy directly from the provider.

    1. EnergyFrom token type and published inference data

    3. WaterCooling plus water used to make the electricity

    How Tend handles uncertainty, funding, and privacy
    Each figure comes with a range
    Model efficiency, hardware, location, electricity, cooling, and weather all change the result. Until providers publish more granular data, Tend shows the range.
    Funding sits beside your footprint
    Removal or restoration doesn't make the original footprint disappear. Tend records what you estimated, what you funded, and the evidence.
    Nothing you write leaves your computer
    Tend runs locally and uses token counts, model names, timestamps, and the projects you track.
